# Zombie Rage — Client Rebuild

The existing Cinematic V4 build is not accepted as the final visual standard.

## Required acceptance criteria
- Original hero; no resemblance to Hulk or other protected characters.
- Upright third-person / 2.5D presentation. Never rotate full-body sprites flat around the canvas.
- Zombies remain visibly upright and use walk/run attack motion.
- Strong cinematic title screen with original hero, horde, burning ruined city, depth, lighting, smoke and fire.
- Gameplay camera/presentation must communicate depth rather than a flat top-down board.
- Multiple visually distinct enemy classes: walker, runner, brute, boss.
- Professional HUD: health, rage, wave, kills, weapon/ammo, pause.
- Mobile controls: movement joystick, aim/fire control, rage ability.
- Combat feedback: muzzle flash, recoil, hit flash, blood/debris, screen shake for heavy impacts.
- Environment: road/street geometry, wrecks, barricades, rubble, fire, shadows and atmospheric effects.
- Difficulty selector: Easy / Normal / Hard.
- Game-over screen must match the visual quality of the title screen.
- Preserve playable wave loop and progressive weapons.

Do not label a build finished merely because it deploys. It must satisfy the visual criteria above in the playable game.
