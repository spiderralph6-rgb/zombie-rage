# Zombie Rage — Visual Upgrade

Active production pass on `visual-upgrade`.

## Locked direction
- Keep genuine third-person WebGL gameplay; never regress to 2D.
- Replace primitive/mannequin presentation with a rugged tactical survivor and frightening infected silhouettes.
- Increase ruined-city density: wrecks, barricades, debris, fires, smoke, fog, signage and street lighting.
- Improve combat readability: stronger muzzle flash, tracers, impact sparks, blood feedback, recoil and headshot feedback.
- Keep the scene brighter and readable on Android while retaining horror atmosphere.
- Protect mobile performance with bounded particles, geometry and shadow use.

## Acceptance gate before production
Menu, scene render, movement, touch aim, manual fire/misses, body/head hits, enemy damage, waves, boss, rage, game over/restart, landscape layout and public deployment must be verified before `main` is replaced.
